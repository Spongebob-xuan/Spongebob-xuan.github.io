I’m Jinxuan Hu, currently pursuing a Master in Financial Engineering at Cornell University, and I hold a B.S. in Mathematics from Nanjing University.

My past internships include roles at Lingjun Investment, Sinolink Securities, and Huatai Futures, where I focused on quantitative research, options trading, and high-frequency backtesting. I built models using Python and financial data APIs, working on alpha signal generation, return prediction, and execution strategies.

At Cornell, I’m advancing my skills in stochastic processes, statistical learning, and derivatives pricing, with a focus on quantitative trading and risk management. I’m especially interested in the intersection of machine learning and financial markets.

Outside of work, I enjoy photography and traveling, and I’m always up for a good soccer match!
